# 4 · Verify: the gate

This stage applies the new-row gate in `research-standards.md` ("A row added after review gets every check…").
- Every check runs in a fresh Opus agent, one account per agent. Prompts are in `agent-briefs.md`.
- Agents report; the main session decides and writes.

| Check | What it must establish | What it changed before |
|---|---|---|
| Oracle second pass, each Yes | The agent sees only the claim and its cited source, searches first, then runs the five disproof tests. Verdict: confirmed, narrowed or failed. | NextEra's Unifier was narrowed: use is public, our delivery rests on one bare deck line. "We delivered" became "on our Oracle credentials list (scope to confirm)". |
| Edge AI Yes | Every evidence item has its own date and source. Vendor names come only from the source that names them. Note where the AI runs. | NextEra's May 2026 story named no vendors. Hailo-8 support sits in QNX SDP 8.0, not the safety OS. |
| Signals, each one | Open the source and check the quote, the date (posting date, and open or closed today) and the team it comes from. Regrade against the rubric. A closed ad is "sought", an open one "hiring". An ad from another function stays listed as W with its reason (the ALSO precedent). | BlackBerry's 10-K line went from S to M (company-wide). NextEra's hook was a Feb 2025 ad, too old to count. |
| Fit facts | Each two-pager claim gets a dated source. The likely problem gets a cost of delay, with its arithmetic marked inferred. | "Largest wind and solar fleet" turned out to be old boilerplate. TI had no 2026 QNX BSP. |
| Internal claims | Each is traced to its file and slide, or to Alex's message, and credited to whoever said it. Client status is worded exactly: "SoftServe client" only when the record shows a team or revenue; never "former" on one CRM flag alone. | A colleague's sim-to-real remark had been credited to the AM. Brain Corp's "former client" rested on one CRM flag. |
| Titles | Sales Navigator against public sources. Use neutral wording when they differ. | Justin Moon is SVP on LinkedIn and VP in QNX's releases. |
| Better hook | Search for a current trigger from the team the offer serves: a hire, a mandate, budget, or pain in the exact work. | Duane Arnold's control-room upgrade replaced a stale ad. Generac's AI-transformation hire replaced a CEO quote. |

**After the agents report**
- Apply every correction to the doc fields before stage 5.
- Set `oracle_use.cross_checked: true` and record each item's verdict.
- Write down every claim that was narrowed or dropped. These go into the report.
