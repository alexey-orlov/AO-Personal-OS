# 4 · Verify: the gate

This applies the new-row gate in `research-standards.md`.
- One fresh Opus agent per check, using `briefs-verify.md`.
- Agents report; the main session decides and writes.

| Check | Must establish | It caught |
|---|---|---|
| Oracle, each Yes | A second pass that sees only the claim and its source, then runs the five disproof tests. Verdict: confirmed, narrowed or failed. | NextEra's Unifier was narrowed. "We delivered" became "on our Oracle credentials list (scope to confirm)". |
| Edge AI, Yes | Each item has its own date and source; vendor names come only from the source that names them; where the AI runs. | Hailo-8 support sat in SDP 8.0, not the safety OS. |
| Each signal | Open the source: the quote, the date, open or closed, which team. Regrade. A closed ad is "sought"; an ad from another function stays as W with its reason. | BlackBerry's 10-K line went from S to M. A Feb 2025 ad was NextEra's hook. |
| Fit facts | A dated source for each two-pager claim, plus a cost of delay with its arithmetic marked inferred. | "Largest wind and solar fleet" was old boilerplate. TI had no 2026 QNX BSP. |
| Internal claims | Traced to the file and slide, or to Alex's message, and credited to whoever said it. "SoftServe client" only when the record shows a team or revenue; never "former" on one CRM flag. | A colleague's remark had been credited to the AM. |
| Titles | Sales Navigator against public sources; neutral wording when they differ. | Justin Moon: SVP on LinkedIn, VP in QNX's releases. |
| Better hook | A current trigger from the team the offer serves: a hire, a mandate, budget, or pain in the exact work. | Duane Arnold's control-room upgrade replaced a stale ad. |

**After the agents report**
- Apply the corrections.
- Set `oracle_use.cross_checked: true` and record each item's verdict.
- List everything narrowed or dropped for the report.
