# Agent briefs: research (stage 2)

Prefix each with `briefs-common.md`; shapes from `templates/account-doc.json`.

**Company, units and roles**
> Write `what_they_do` (one short paragraph), `sum_what`, `hq_text`, `hq_california` and `headcount` (latest annual report, quoted).
> List every R&D unit and what it builds or researches, in concrete nouns: products, devices, chips, modalities. Label each F or I, and write `sum_units_*`.
> Map the personas in `context.md` onto the company's own org (buyer, champion, co-sponsor, gatekeeper), each with a one-line why.
> Name the current holders of those roles from public sources only.
> Give a fit verdict for each offer, with the evidence.

**Signals**
> Find adoption (`+`) and pain (`!`) signals from the team the offer serves, in the places listed in `context.md`.
> Grade each S, M or W with the rubric.
> List them strongest first and write `sum_signals_*`.
> Name the best hook: a current public sign that AI is on that team's radar.

**Oracle**
> Confirmed use only: Oracle, the company or a named partner says so, or the company's own job ads from the last 18 months name the system.
> Also record what the company runs instead, any migrations, and each item not counted, with why.

**Edge AI**
> Does the company build a device, or an app on a device, whose AI runs on the device or splits with the cloud? Use the kit definition in `context.md`.
> Give dated evidence (shipped, planned or hiring) and say where the AI runs.
> Name vendors only from the source that names them.
> Map the kit's layers that apply; note caveats.

**Size: first principles and public**
> R&D spend or headcount × the share in the target work, as low, likely and high, with the arithmetic.
> Plus any public team figure, sourced.

Every agent also lists, in its output, each search a limit or a blocked page left undone.
