# Agent briefs: verify (stage 4), each in a fresh agent

Prefix each with `briefs-common.md`. The input file holds the row's research.

**Second pass on a Yes** (each Oracle product, the Edge AI verdict, the offer choice)
> Try to disprove this claim; confirm it only if evidence survives. Claim: <…>. Cited source: <…>.
> Search on your own first, then test for: a job-ad line naming it only as a skill; a copy not traceable to the company; an undated page; evidence older than 18 months; a newer migration.
> Quote `decks.txt` exactly for an internal source. For the offer choice, also test the rejected offer: does no team really need it?
> Verdict: confirmed, narrowed or failed, with evidence items (url, date, quote, check) and each test's result.

**Signals**
> For every signal in `<input.json>`, confirm the quote, the true date (a job ad: open or closed today) and its team.
> A careers-page redirect proves nothing; date a closure from a dated copy.
> Regrade with the rubric; recommend keep, regrade or drop, with reason and corrected text. A closed ad is "sought".
> Leave Sales Navigator items unopened: confirm them on the public web, graded on their text.

**Facts and the hook**
> Each fact the two-pager will print (identity, HQ, sizes, programs, units, people in `<input.json>`) gets a verdict (correct, wrong, outdated or unsupported), a dated source and corrected wording.
> Price the likely problem's cost to the business, arithmetic marked "(inferred)".
> Confirm each champion's title in a public source, or say none exists.
> Name the strongest current public sign that AI is on the radar of <the team the offer serves> (a hire, mandate, program or budget) and the strongest pain trigger in the exact work, or say plainly none exists.
