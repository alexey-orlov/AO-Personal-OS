## Pause

- The one real break, 19.08 → 14.09 (26 days): the trainer kept the top
  weights (ГАКК 85→120 vs 100/120/120 before, Жим лёжа 80, Сведение 73, Смитт
  40) and only started lower; reps fell a little (Жим лёжа 8-8-6). Pullover
  was the exception: 45-50-50 → 36-41-45, and it stayed reduced (41) after.
- 07.10: Жим сидя 61-66-70 after 58 days off that machine, chest trained
  weekly meanwhile → time away from the muscle group, not from one machine,
  drives the cut.
- Rule: up to 5 weeks off a group keeps the weight (first set lighter, reps
  at the bottom); 5–8 weeks −10 %, 8–13 −20 %, longer −30 %. Lower-back
  caution exercises −10 % after 3 weeks (the pullover case; ГАКК and жим
  ногами included by best practice, more conservative than the trainer).
- New gym (US from 2026-11-19): other machines, other levers and lb stacks.
  First time per machine = Kyiv weight × 0.9, floored to the 5-lb grid,
  first set two steps lighter (03.08: a new Smith press started at 50 and
  had to drop to 40).

## Test rounds

- Round 1 (2026-11-23, six fresh-context agents running the skill as
  written: first US session, back pain, four sessions with feedback, bad
  sleep + 40 min + missing machine, back day without T-bar, blind replay of
  16.10). All plans passed `check`; the blind replay matched the trainer's
  16.10 in 5 of 6 exercises and weights, 2 of 3 warm-up movements and the
  crossfit shape. Fixed after it: transfer factors and probe; the calibration
  probe rule and the step shown in the table; substitutions actually read;
  time and readiness trimming in the engine; warm-up trio and crossfit
  doses from the engine (the agents had to guess both); recent pain carried
  to the next session; cues only when relevant; per-hand / per-leg reps;
  implement names in lb that never exceed a cap (10 kg extension was a
  25-lb plate); text format for Telegram; contradictions in the method text.

- Round 2 (2026-11-23, two fresh agents on the rewritten engine: three
  sessions with feedback and a missing T-bar; back pain + bad sleep + 45 min +
  a busy machine). Both passed on the lower back, the pause, the format,
  kg + lb and names. Fixed after it: a probe exercise without a weight
  dropped out of the next template (broke the superset); «колено нормально»
  could not clear a pain flag (now `--ok`); a floor hinge (squat high pull)
  sat in the crossfit pool (retired); crossfit loads came from the catalog
  instead of the trainer's last ones; a busy machine had no substitute
  (presses and rows got `alt_ids`); the brief now trims to the time itself,
  asks the pain screen, and check accepts warnings justified in `overrides`
  and estimates the whole session.
