A confidential, high-growth global software organization with approximately 3,200 employees and annual recurring revenue exceeding $650 million is accelerating its next phase of product-led growth. The company operates across 55+ countries, serving over 10,000 enterprise customers in the HR technology, talent acquisition, and workforce management sectors.

Headquartered in the San Francisco Bay Area, with significant European and APAC operations, the organization has recently secured a $200 million growth equity investment from a tier-1 private equity firm. This investment is fueling a strategic expansion of AI-native capabilities, ecosystem partnerships, and platform extensibility designed to disrupt the traditional HR tech landscape.

The company has evolved from a single-tenant legacy platform to a multi-tenant, AI-enabled SaaS platform over the last five years. Today, it is recognized as a leader in the Gartner Magic Quadrant for Talent Management, with a reputation for product innovation, customer-centric design, and strong unit economics.

### Why This Position Has Been Created

This is a newly established executive role, created to drive the next phase of the company's product evolution. Leadership recognized the need for a dedicated product executive to:

- Lead AI-first product innovation across the core platform, including generative AI agents, predictive analytics, and intelligent automation
- Build and scale the ecosystem strategy, including APIs, marketplace, embedded partner solutions, and developer enablement areas where you've had direct success at Creatio and Jooble
- Unify product, engineering, and GTM functions to accelerate time-to-market for AI-powered features that drive enterprise adoption and retention

The role sits at the intersection of product strategy, technology innovation, and commercial impact. It is not a replacement it is a deliberate investment in the company's future as an AI-native platform.

### The Opportunity

Reporting directly to the Chief Product Officer, the Vice President, Product – AI & Ecosystem will serve as a key member of the Product Leadership Team and the broader Executive Leadership Group.

This role carries full P&L responsibility for the AI product portfolio and ecosystem business line, with direct accountability for:

- Product Roadmap & Vision: Defining a 2–3 year product strategy for AI features and ecosystem capabilities that differentiates the platform in the market
- AI Product Portfolio: Leading the ideation, development, and launch of AI-powered features including conversational AI agents, candidate matching algorithms, workflow automation, and predictive intelligence
- Ecosystem & Platform Growth: Expanding the partner ecosystem through APIs, marketplace integrations, and developer tooling mirroring your experience leading ecosystem products at Creatio
- Cross-Functional Leadership: Partnering with Engineering, Sales, Marketing, and Customer Success to align product priorities with business outcomes and customer needs
- M&A Integration: Supporting future acquisition opportunities by ensuring seamless product integration and platform consistency
- Talent Development: Building and mentoring a high-performing product team of 25+ product managers, designers, and analysts
