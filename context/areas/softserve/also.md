  - *Product firmware (the ECUs' own code).* **Coyote power faults:** besides the loss of propulsion, reserve-battery charging problems, CAN going offline at low charge and no charging from 0%; the planned firmware release fixes only part of the set, and the investigations are cross-team, not code alone. **Fault response:** Manatee's diagnostic review finds the shared fault framework mostly reports faults without triggering the intended protective responses; pre-charge fault gating and wake-up inhibition are unfinished (hardware safety paths and the BMS control model's protections are separate). **Tests:** the same CI gaps, plus gaps in controller-application and CAN integration testing in Manatee's test strategy (an October baseline). **Firmware, Controls and Systems boundaries (Manatee):** power and vehicle-state policy, fault recovery, safe stop vs immediate torque-off and calibration ownership are undecided; the ownership-split page is a proposal, not an agreement, and notes torque commands forwarded once a second where 10 ms is required. **Release regressions:** a security change removed a capability needed to replace a drive unit in the field (fixed in main, not yet released), so factory firmware, service tooling and mixed C/D modules must be validated together.
