## Customer context
- Payworks is adopting a new AI-assisted product process built around Claude projects: each **initiative** (e.g., HR management, payroll, expense management) gets a scaffolded Claude project that PMs fill with context files.
- Flow: PM runs **initiative discovery** → produces a **product brief** (persona, problem, business-outcome link, end-to-end workflow map, AI-reimagination step, opportunity-solution tree, recruited design partners = power users, not UX designers) → builds a **prototype** (front-end only, no backend/APIs) used as the source of truth in place of PRDs → breaks the prototype into **micro-jobs** (independently valuable milestones, larger than a sprint increment; ~POC/MVP/full-product staging) → hands high-fidelity micro-job prototypes to BA and Dev.
- Maturity: the process is new to Payworks — run end-to-end only ~2–3 times, not 15; they are actively iterating on what works. (stated)
- Prior AI adoption appears ad hoc (no systematic use of Claude projects before these test initiatives). (inferred — flagged by Speaker B as something to confirm)
