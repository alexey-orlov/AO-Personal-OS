## 1. The ask, in one paragraph

Oracle wants a sellable **menu of AI use cases for NATO countries — military forces and miltech companies — that runs on Oracle's *distributed cloud***, ideally backed by SoftServe's delivered Ukraine war work, with a Nemotron angle from NVIDIA. Two Oracle buyers are in play: **the VP in charge of Oracle's NATO activities** (the NCIA→OCI account) and **Oracle's EMEA Leader for Public**, who "also would like to add our use cases". Karsten's selection rule: **"the real compelling ones, with the greatest demand."** Customer segment is undefined by design; the named gap is **no reusable assets** — only the six FreeTech cases. → [report §1 (frame)](2026-09-03_nato-ai-use-case-map.md#1-the-frame--what-oracle-asked-and-what-constrains-the-answer) · stakeholder table in [oracle-defense](../oracle-defense.md) and [oracle-team](../oracle-team.md)

## 2. The map — 18 cases in 5 AI archetypes + 1 platform; first wave of 5

**Two levels, one grouping rule** (red-teamed 2026-09-06 in three passes — completeness vs NATO/NCIA software needs · overlaps & naming · FreeTech fit): **L1 = the AI archetype** (the engine family Oracle + NVIDIA ship and the data it works on), **L2 = the use case**, named `<object> + <job>` so the map reads from the names. Buyer (NCIA · NSPA · NATO bodies · nations · miltech), tier and deployment rung are tags, not levels — NCIA's demand turned out to be a *stripe across four archetypes*, not a group. Six cases were added where NCIA / NATO portfolio families had none (enterprise IT & service ops · space / GEOINT · exercises · personnel casework on Fusion HR · the information environment · live edge vision for FreeTech 06). Lead with cases where an **existing OCI AI Accelerator Pack** (or Lakehouse) meets a **FreeTech- or pipeline-proven capability** and an **unclassified / synthetic data path**; keep the kinetic end off the map. → [report §0](2026-09-03_nato-ai-use-case-map.md#0-the-answer-in-one-screen) · [§3.1 the map + segregation rule](2026-09-03_nato-ai-use-case-map.md#31-the-map) · [§3.1.1 red-team log](2026-09-03_nato-ai-use-case-map.md#311-red-team-log-2026-09-06--three-passes-what-each-changed) · [§9 cards](2026-09-03_nato-ai-use-case-map.md#9-the-18-cases--detail-cards) · [§10 bench](2026-09-03_nato-ai-use-case-map.md#10-bench--kept-off-the-map-kept-for-the-brainstorm) · **slides:** "NATO AI use-case map.pptx" (SoftServe template; twelve slides — a selection-criteria opener, the NATO demand signals, the FreeTech delivered cases, the neutral map, the first-wave map (orange, plus 4.2 in Oracle red as the delivered Bosch reference), six case slides with an identical problem → solution → integration-diagram → proof structure naming the stack as OCI · AI Lakehouse · AIDP · NeMo + AI-Q · NeMo + VSS · NeMo, and a closing next-steps slide (discovery with Oracle); v10 delivered 2026-09-07 → OneDrive `Projects/Oracle/NATO/`) · **AIDP re-checked case by case + Patterns v2 gap list 2026-09-07** → [report §3.1.4](2026-09-03_nato-ai-use-case-map.md#314-aidp-double-check-and-the-patterns-v2-coverage-check-2026-09-07)
