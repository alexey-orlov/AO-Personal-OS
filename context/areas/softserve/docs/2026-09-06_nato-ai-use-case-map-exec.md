## 1. The ask, in one paragraph

Oracle wants a sellable **menu of AI use cases for NATO countries — military forces and miltech companies — that runs on Oracle's *distributed cloud***, ideally backed by SoftServe's delivered Ukraine war work, with a Nemotron angle from NVIDIA. Two Oracle buyers are in play: **the VP in charge of Oracle's NATO activities** (the NCIA→OCI account) and **Oracle's EMEA Leader for Public**, who "also would like to add our use cases". Karsten's selection rule: **"the real compelling ones, with the greatest demand."** Customer segment is undefined by design; the named gap is **no reusable assets** — only the six FreeTech cases. → [report §1 (frame)](2026-09-03_nato-ai-use-case-map.md#1-the-frame--what-oracle-asked-and-what-constrains-the-answer) · stakeholder table in [oracle-defense](../oracle-defense.md) and [oracle-team](../oracle-team.md)

## 3. What we actually know (facts, not inference)

- **Oracle at NATO = the enterprise tier inside a multi-cloud NCIA.** NCIA's ERP is Oracle E-Business Suite + Fusion; NCIA selected OCI in Sep 2025 for "mission-critical workloads" and the migration of three legacy data centres (prime Thales, Red Reply / Shield Reply, Proximus; Oracle voice Richard Smith, EVP & GM EMEA; no NCIA quote, no value, term, region type or classification level). The **classified operational tier is Google (JATEC, Nov 2025) and Palantir (Maven Smart System NATO, accredited Jun 2026)**; the €200M Protected Business Network runs "across a multi-cloud environment provided by NCIA". **No NATO RESTRICTED/SECRET accreditation is public for any Oracle offering.** → Never repeat "Oracle is the major private cloud for NATO" to a NATO buyer. → [§4.1–4.2](2026-09-03_nato-ai-use-case-map.md#41-verified-nato-side-oracle-footprint) · [NCIA release](https://www.oracle.com/uk/news/announcement/nato-communications-and-information-agency-selects-oci-2025-09-11/)
- **"Distributed cloud" is the target environment** (Karsten's email): Dedicated Region (L40S–GB200) · Alloy · Government & Isolated regions · Compute Cloud@Customer Isolated (air-gapped, 6–8-week fast-start, 4–48 L40S) · Roving Edge (3× L4). EU Sovereign Cloud (Frankfurt/Madrid) already runs OCI GenAI; the UK MoD is on Oracle since 2026-01-14. → [§4.3](2026-09-03_nato-ai-use-case-map.md#43-oracles-defence--sovereign-line-a-partner-can-build-on)
- **NATO's own demand list** = DIANA challenge areas (2026: 10 areas incl. sensing, contested EM, logistics, autonomy, data-assisted decision-making; 2027: sensing & data processing for ISR, responsive logistics, multidomain autonomy, air-defence countermeasures), the revised 2024 AI Strategy and its six Principles of Responsible Use, the Rapid Adoption Action Plan (24-month adoption target). Every funded source splits **mission vs enterprise — and the enterprise half is larger** (GenAI.mil 3M seats; Task Force Lima ~60% chatbot-shaped). Karsten's five topics omit enterprise, logistics and cyber — the three most PoV-friendly clusters. → [§3.3](2026-09-03_nato-ai-use-case-map.md#33-how-primary-sources-cluster-defence-ai--and-the-reconciliation-with-karstens-5-topics) · [§3.4 named instances + funding](2026-09-03_nato-ai-use-case-map.md#34-named-instances-and-202526-funding-per-cluster-the-x-already-does-this-column) · [§4.4](2026-09-03_nato-ai-use-case-map.md#44-natos-ai-direction-what-a-partners-menu-must-visibly-align-with)
- **Nemotron on OCI is import-only** onto a Dedicated AI Cluster (Super since Mar 2026, Ultra needs B200_X4, Nano Omni in OCI Enterprise AI) — a cluster line item in every PoV price; no "Nemotron for Government" SKU; AWS GovCloud already runs Nemotron at FedRAMP High / IL5 while OCI US-Gov "plans to host". **The two NVIDIA decks are general customer decks with no Public & Defense content** (NVIDIA-confidential); they add the sovereignty narrative, the runtime blocks (OpenShell, NeMo Switchyard, NeMo Relay, NemoClaw) and the CrowdStrike cyber reference. → [§3.2 + §3.2.1](2026-09-03_nato-ai-use-case-map.md#32-nvidia-building-blocks-on-oci--what-is-verified-vs-assumed)
- **SoftServe's proof sits in the physical/sensor half of the map** (FreeTech: autonomy, intel fusion, RF clustering, control tower, UUV twin, edge vision); **Oracle's packs cover the enterprise/knowledge half** (7 packs: VSS, Knowledge Chat, AI-Q starter kit, Document Extraction, two cuOpt optimizers, managed RAG). The menu's job is to bridge the two. → [§5 FreeTech mapping](2026-09-03_nato-ai-use-case-map.md#5-angle-c--softserves-delivered-defence-base-freetech-one-pager--clusters-proof-reuse) · [§7 packs](2026-09-03_nato-ai-use-case-map.md#7-angle-d--oracle-ai-accelerator-packs--softserve-productized-rows--nato-relevance)
