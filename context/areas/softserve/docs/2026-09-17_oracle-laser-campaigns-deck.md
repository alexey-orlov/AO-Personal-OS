## What a laser campaign is

**"Speed. Simplicity. Repeatability."** Oracle's own diagnosis of its sales problem: sellers face **too many offerings**, **sales plays alone aren't enough**, and **positioning AI is complex with sales cycles too long**. The fix is a **ready-to-go proposition, backed by co-ordinated BDM, DemGen & marketing outreach** — not a slide and not an idea.

Three defining properties:
- **Proven** — focused AI & Data sales plays built on **simple, proven customer use cases**.
- **Supported** — backed by coordinated sales, business development and marketing outreach.
- **Speed** — designed to generate **in-quarter pipeline and in-year bookings**.

**Outcome as Oracle states it:** _"Concentrate sales & marketing effort on the proposition most likely to land with a specific customer."_

**The mechanics — four things bundled so the seller gets a launch-ready motion:**
1. **Repeatable use case** — a simple, proven use case **validated by Product Management**, sellable repeatedly.
2. **Mapped to customers** — the use case matched to customers with the right characteristics **from Oracle's installed base**.
3. **Planned outreach** — structured email and sales outreach with marketing, PMs and sales.
4. **Launch-ready** — ideas loaded into **Goldmines**, assets prepared, customer outreach ready to go.

_Result: "A relevant, pre-validated opening proposition - not a generic AI pitch."_

## The three AI themes Oracle is selling against

| Theme | Oracle's framing | The one-liner |
|---|---|---|
| **Trusted data** | Production AI needs **governed, accessible enterprise data — not just pilot-ready synthetic data** | "Data quality determines AI relevance" |
| **Open & connected AI** | Enterprises need freedom to combine **agents, models and data across clouds — with one control layer** | "A unified intelligence platform enables choice" |
| **AI economics** | As usage grows, organisations must manage **token, infrastructure and operating costs** | "Efficiency will become the next AI advantage" |

Mapped to three solutions: **OCI Enterprise** (agentic workflows across data sources, managed models, sovereign AI options, dedicated infrastructure) · **AI Lakehouse** (open interoperable data access across multi-cloud; run AI on all your data without moving it) · **AI Data Platform** (enterprise data + AI models + agents together to build and run trusted AI at scale).

## The five assets shipped with every campaign

1. **Target segment** — prioritised customer list
2. **Goldmines** — ideas to assign (the CRM tool reps are measured in)
3. **Seller tools** — SharePoint sites, GPTs (a "ChatGPT AI Coach to find info & build a pitch deck")
4. **Ready-to-use assets** — **seller guides, customer sheets**
5. **AI live instance** — _"show not tell"_, run by **ACE champs**

## The proof point Oracle leads with — Qatar Airways, a 4-week AIDP fast track

| Week 1 | Week 1–2 | Week 3 | Week 4 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Executive briefing (EBC)** — frame the business outcome and the golden-question use case with the customer's exec team | **Deep Insights demo** — run the agent on a **governed synthetic model** and answer the golden questions end to end | **Live-data prototype** — build on the live estate; tune the **semantic layer** while pricing and security progress | **Production with Lift** — scale the prototype into production with **Oracle Lift** for a reference-ready deployment |

Headline results: **29/29 golden questions answered live end-to-end · no second POC** (the CFO wanted to move straight to live data) **· 7 live EPM modules in scope**. _"Deep Insights combines cross-source analytics, unstructured data, and multi-step what-ifs."_

## The sales flow Oracle wants sellers to run — "Show don't tell"

| 1. Spark interest | 2. Show the solution | 3. Let them try | 4. Prove the fit | 5. Propose & proceed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Confirm the problem and lead with an outcome | Use **LiveStack demos** with SE & ACE support | Get the client's testing hands; **avoid POCs** | Validate technical fit & business value | Turn proven value into a focused proposal |
| _Seller guides, ChatGPT skill_ | _SEs & ACEs, AI platform team_ | _LiveStack & Codex demos_ | _**Solution design and proof**_ | _**LIFT / CSS engagements**_ |

And the conversation frame: **Understand** (their business problem and desired outcome) → **Explore** (where work is slow, costly, or hard to scale) → **Show** (a relevant example, **involve specialists early — in the first meeting**) → **Advance** (a focused next step to test value). Stated plainly: _"Ideally get them to play with the solution — **workshops and tailored demos are better than Pilots and POCs**."_

**Seller action this week:** choose 3 accounts, open Goldmines, assign their best-fit AI ideas; **book 1 AI discovery conversation**.

## The two things in here that cut against SoftServe's current packaging

1. **"Avoid POCs."** Oracle's own field motion routes **demo → workshop → solution design → LIFT/CSS engagement**, explicitly preferring workshops and tailored demos over pilots and POCs — while SoftServe's pack model is built on a **PoV Jumpstart** first tier and the [2026-09-16 sync](2026-09-16_oracle-neil-gero-partnership-sync.md) simultaneously validated "POC as door opener". Reconcilable (Oracle is trying to kill *unpaid, slow* POCs inside its own sales cycle, and the Neil note's POCs are funded SoftServe engagements), but the **entry-tier language matters**: a "PoV Jumpstart" that reads as a POC will be deprioritised by a rep following this deck, while the same thing named as a **discovery workshop + live demo** rides the motion.
2. **Oracle's own production route is LIFT / CSS, not a partner by default.** Step 5 names **Oracle Lift** and **CSS** as the engagement that follows proof. SoftServe has to be *inserted* at steps 4–5, which is exactly what [Neil described](2026-09-16_oracle-neil-gero-partnership-sync.md) as the earliest reasonable insertion point — and exactly what the quotable S/M/L price points are supposed to make automatic.
