## Where in the motion, not just which segment

Oracle's flow is **Spark → Show → Let them try → Prove the fit → Propose & proceed**, with Oracle's own default landing on **LIFT / CSS** at step 5. SoftServe has to be inserted, and the deck says exactly where the openings are:

- **Step 2 "Show the solution" / asset 5 "AI live instance — show not tell."** Our three **interactive walkthroughs** (Large docs · Workforce optimization · Cross-system ERP Q&A) are already built, brand-free and synthetic. They are the cheapest thing to hand a rep and the only asset class we have that Oracle is explicitly short of. *Offer them as laser-campaign assets.*
- **Asset 4 "Ready-to-use assets — seller guides, customer sheets."** This is the slot the **quotable S/M/L price points** fill. Neil's ask and the campaign asset list are the same ask seen from two ends.
- **Step 4 "Prove the fit — solution design and proof."** Neil's stated insertion point ("when the customer wants to experiment… still pre-sales"). This is where the paid entry tier lands.
- **Step 5 "Propose & proceed."** Oracle Lift / CSS today; the partner-tier upgrade, the pod model and the dedicated practice are all aimed at making SoftServe the alternative here.

## The naming problem worth fixing before anything else ships

The deck tells reps to **avoid POCs** and says **"workshops and tailored demos are better than Pilots and POCs"** — while our entry tier is named **PoV Jumpstart** and the Neil sync separately validated "POC as door opener". These are reconcilable (Oracle is killing slow unpaid POCs inside *its* cycle; ours are funded engagements), but a rep reading this deck will skip anything that reads like a POC. The entry tier should present as a **discovery workshop + live demo with a fixed price and a fixed clock**, with the PoV mechanics underneath — which is also exactly the **parallel AI discovery/ideation workshop** Neil asked SoftServe to scope for every POC, and the shape Hammad has been pushing since 2026-08-18.
