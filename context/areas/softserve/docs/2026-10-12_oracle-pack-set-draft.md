_Internal draft for the Oracle partnership: which packs to build on Oracle AI Data Platform (AIDP, with or without AI Lakehouse) and on OCI + NVIDIA NeMo. Up to 8 new packs besides the existing ones. PDF twin: [2026-10-12_oracle-pack-set-draft.pdf](2026-10-12_oracle-pack-set-draft.pdf)._
_How it was made: parallel research on AIDP use cases, NeMo-on-OCI use cases and the internal inventory (mini-site content, laser segments, the [22 Sep segment analysis](2026-09-22_laser-segments-softserve-fit.md), [Oscar's AIDP sessions](../oracle-aidp.md) and today's [AIDP layer slide](2026-10-12_aidp-telenor-demo-screens.md#08--oracle-slide-the-aidp-architecture-122157--1753)); three independent set designs merged into one; three red-team rounds (business reader, technical reader with fact-check, boundaries, plain language, brief compliance) plus fresh readers who saw only the page. Oracle web pages returned 403 to the research agents, so some Oracle facts rest on search snippets and the wiki: re-verify before client use._

The 9 products we have (5 rows; 3 more share their engines) and 6 new (5 packs, 1 service), within the cap of 8 new. Together they cover the distinct uses of AI Data Platform and Oracle Cloud (OCI) + NVIDIA, all six mini-site groups and Oracle's four priority laser campaigns.

## Context

**What AIDP is used for (5 distinct cases)**

- **A. Why did the number move.** A CFO asks why company revenue, cost or margin missed budget; an agent ranks the causes in finance data and proposes an action. Pack: P&L variance analysis.
- **B. Agents that prepare fixes in the ERP.** Agents watch E-Business Suite for held invoices, late orders and slow stock and prepare each fix for approval. Pack: EBS exception agents.
- **C. Answers and fields from documents.** Questions over contracts and policies in cloud storage, answered with the page cited; key fields pulled into tables. Packs: Document Q&A, Large docs.
- **D. One consistent data layer.** Old warehouses and other databases linked or copied into AI Lakehouse so every report gives the same number: step one of Business metrics Q&A. Moves off Databricks: per deal.
- **E. Predictions for every customer or product.** Machine-learning scores on lakehouse data: who will leave, what will sell. Pack: Customer churn and retention.

**What OCI + NVIDIA (NeMo, blueprints) is used for (5 distinct cases)**

- **1. Cited research across many sources.** A multi-step investigation across documents and systems, written up as a cited report (NVIDIA AI-Q blueprint). Packs: Plan vs actual, Account insights, Case evidence collection.
- **2. Document-to-record extraction.** Fields from long documents loaded into a system, each linked to its page (NeMo Retriever). Pack: Large docs.
- **3. Video and image understanding.** Events found in footage, damage judged from photos (NVIDIA video search blueprint, Cosmos Reason vision model). Pack: Repair-or-replace.
- **4. Customer agents by voice.** A caller talks to an agent that answers and completes simple requests (Nemotron speech; NVIDIA's blueprint is still a developer example). Pack: the voice tier of Customer service agent.
- **5. Own tuned models instead of a paid AI service.** An open model tuned on the company's own traffic gives the same answers for less (NeMo Customizer, NIM). Service: AI running-cost reduction.

_Case 3 runs on NVIDIA's video stack, not NeMo. Route and roster planning (NVIDIA cuOpt, also not NeMo) runs on OCI GPUs too: Workforce and Fleet._

**How Oracle positions AIDP.** Oracle draws AIDP in four layers: company data where it sits; a business map of it (ontology); a workbench to build agents, plus its own analyst agent (Deep Insights) and action agents with a person approving; then answers in chat, workflows and apps. It sells an outcome to the CFO: a demo on sample data in 2-3 days, production in 4-5 weeks, deals of $1M+.

**What an AIDP pack is.** Where Oracle ships the agent (Deep Insights, EBS agents), our pack is the demo kit (questions, sample data, expected answers, the money case) plus the rollout; we build only where Oracle ships nothing. OCI packs are our own software, mostly on NVIDIA blueprints. Oracle asks us not to combine NVIDIA GPU work with AIDP yet, so no pack mixes the two.

**Laser campaigns.** Oracle aims 13 laser campaigns at EMEA accounts by what they run (L = AI Lakehouse, A = AIDP, O = OCI AI; figure = accounts). Priority: L1 Lakehouse (225), A1 E-Business Suite (123), O1 Digital Assistant (70), O3 AI running cost (189+), each with a row below. Our 22 Sep segment proposals are rows 2, 3, 4, 8 and 11.

**Mini-site groups.** All six get a pack: knowledge & analytics, deep research, documents, transactions & processes (empty today, now two), forecasting & optimization, video & image.

**What we keep.** All nine stay; six head a row (row 1 holds both metrics Q&A listings). Three reuse an engine: Account insights (news turned into sales openings) and Case evidence collection (a cited file per complaint), AI-Q like row 6; Fleet route optimization (today's van routes), cuOpt like row 9. Not proposed: demand forecasting (churn's scoring covers it), a data-migration pack.

## Which pack gets the request

- "What is the number?": Business metrics Q&A. "Why did company margin miss budget?": P&L variance. "Why did this project overrun?": Plan vs actual.
- A quick answer quoted from files: Document Q&A. A cited report across files and systems: an AI-Q pack. The same fields from every file, loaded into a system: Large docs.
- Staff fixing ERP transactions: EBS exception agents. Outside customers by chat or voice: Customer service agent. A working AI app that costs too much per request: AI running-cost reduction.
- Who will cancel, judged from their own billing and usage: Churn. Which outside news opens a sale at an account: Account insights.
