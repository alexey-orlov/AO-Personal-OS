# AI Jumpstart program for scientists

_status: in design for launch: a three-person team (Bohdan · Alex · Inna) is building the program deck for a session this week (Alex may present 2026-10-20). Target verticals are pharma / drug discovery, chemical manufacturing and energy; the start is a free lighthouse client per person, run as discovery. Alex's problems × tooling map and exec summary are in his review. No client, date or pricing yet._
_updated: 2026-10-19_

## Snapshot

- **What it is:** the third Jumpstart track after SDLC (engineering) and PM, for research teams, on the same enablement model: team collaboration · a shared knowledge layer · shared skills and sub-agents that, with MCP integrations, form pipelines that automate the work. Offering-design history for the other tracks: [jumpstart-pm](jumpstart-pm.md). (chat, 2026-10-19)
- **Target verticals (2026-10-19):** pharma and drug discovery (small and large molecules alike) · chemical manufacturing (adhesives, DuPont-type manufacturers) · energy (cut off at the recording start; named in Inna's slides and Alex's recap, inferred). One program aims to cover all three. Chosen from Bohdan's three presales as a life-science expert and from how partners (Microsoft Discovery, NVIDIA) and the competitor NobleAI pitch, not from market research. Enterprises, not ISVs. [sync](calls/jumpstart-science/2026-10-19_201609_default_pasted-science-jumpstart-sync.md)
- **Program shape (Bohdan's answer to Alex's open questions):** the PM-track model (upskilling + knowledge as the foundation) with **verticalized skills over the scientist's lifecycle**: form a hypothesis → gather what the test needs → test it in the lab. Manufacturing and production optimization (digital twin) is reachable but not the focus. [sync](calls/jumpstart-science/2026-10-19_201609_default_pasted-science-jumpstart-sync.md)
- **Partners:** only platforms big enough to bring leads: **Microsoft Discovery** (established partnership and a funded enterprise sales machine; Inna: the most relevant, partner-team requests already coming), **Claude Science**, **OpenAI's science workbench**, **NVIDIA** (AI-Q for research, open-source drug-discovery models). That is three closed-source and one open-source option. AWS and Google are still to research. The program is positioned as tied to no single partner, because Jumpstart demand came from clients' own stacks. **NobleAI is the competitor**: domain knowledge but weak, vibe-coded software and an aggressive forward-deployed-engineering model that leaves partners only cloud and program management. [sync](calls/jumpstart-science/2026-10-19_201609_default_pasted-science-jumpstart-sync.md)
- **Commercial stance:** always sell transformation, offer cheaper packages; NobleAI's offer, a 5-month engagement (target set in month 1, something working by month 5), is the starting model. Alex's Payworks lesson behind it: client success came only with transformation, which SoftServe didn't deliver there. [sync](calls/jumpstart-science/2026-10-19_201609_default_pasted-science-jumpstart-sync.md)
- **Inclusion rule (Alex):** keep only use cases that plain ChatGPT or Claude, even with Projects, can't do, or does poorly or slowly. (chat, 2026-10-19)
- **Positioning evidence:** the vendor science workbenches cover one scientist's literature work, molecular modeling and analysis. None runs a literature and patent watch, picks next experiments from run history, drives lab automation, keeps PLM/ALM records, assembles regulatory dossiers or captures team routines, and only Microsoft Discovery gives a team a shared knowledge base. That uncovered ground is the track's positioning. → [exec summary](../../../outputs/jumpstart-science/jumpstart-science-exec-summary.md)
- Possibly tied to the Life Sciences and Energy focus domains that landed on the R&D unit in August (inferred; [README](README.md#active-threads-area-level)).
