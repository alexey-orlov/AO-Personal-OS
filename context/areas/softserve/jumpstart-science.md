## Snapshot

- **What it is:** the third Jumpstart track after SDLC (engineering) and PM, for research teams, on the same enablement model: team collaboration · a shared knowledge layer · shared skills and sub-agents that, with MCP integrations, form pipelines that automate the work. Offering-design history for the other tracks: [jumpstart-pm](jumpstart-pm.md). (chat, 2026-10-19) Possibly tied to the Life Sciences and Energy focus domains that landed on the R&D unit in August (inferred; [README](README.md#active-threads-area-level)).
- **Target verticals (2026-10-19):** pharma and drug discovery (small and large molecules alike) · chemical manufacturing (adhesives, DuPont-type manufacturers) · energy (cut off at the recording start; named in Inna's slides and Alex's recap, inferred). One program aims to cover all three. Chosen from Bohdan's three presales as a life-science expert and from how partners (Microsoft Discovery, NVIDIA) and the competitor NobleAI pitch, not from market research. Enterprises, not ISVs. [sync](calls/jumpstart-science/2026-10-19_201609_default_pasted-science-jumpstart-sync.md) Alex's exec summary frames the market as four industry groups: the three targets fold into life sciences and materials & process, while engineered products and AI & data research cover R&D outside them. → [exec summary](../../../outputs/jumpstart-science/jumpstart-science-exec-summary.md)
- **Program shape (Bohdan's answer to Alex's open questions):** the PM-track model (upskilling + knowledge as the foundation) with **verticalized skills over the scientist's lifecycle**: form a hypothesis → gather what the test needs → test it in the lab. Manufacturing and production optimization (digital twin) is reachable but not the focus. [sync](calls/jumpstart-science/2026-10-19_201609_default_pasted-science-jumpstart-sync.md)
- **Delivery design (exec summary page 4, 2026-10-20):** two modes, as on the PM track. **Light (training):** 1 week of prep + 6 weeks: discovery in week 1, picks in weeks 2–5, measurement in week 6. **Full (transformation):** 2 weeks of prep + 11 weeks: discovery in weeks 1–3, picks in weeks 3–10, readout and hand-over in week 11; then advisory for up to 4 weeks (weeks 12–15) and a next-steps workshop (week 16). The weekly motion is the PM track's: SoftServe and the champions build in week N-1, the team is shown it in week N and practises it in real work, and participants show what they achieved in week N+1. The foundation, bucket 6 (the shared knowledge base and skill set), always comes first. A pick is one specific pain inside a bucket: the page-1 use cases are starting points, and teams skip what doesn't hurt. A heavy pick (instruments, heavy compute) gets a deep-dive after discovery and a longer build. Discovery runs longer than on the PM track: per sub-team it covers workflows, roles, data, systems, timelines and bottlenecks, plus each person's AI use and where it fails. (chat, 2026-10-20)
- **Gates, client time and SoftServe team (page 4, 2026-10-20):** before week 1 the team is named, systems inventoried and access requested; by the end of week 3 every pick has an owner, a baseline, a target and open or exported data; every week the sponsor gets a one-page status and a blocked pick swaps with the next one; in week 11 the champions run the setup alone. Client hours a week, Light / Full: sponsor 1 / 1 · champions (one per sub-team) 4 / 6 · early adopters (5–10, Full only) 3 · system owners 1 / 2 · whole team 1 / 1. SoftServe FTE, Light / Full: engagement lead 0.25 / 0.5 · solution architect 0.5 / 0.75 · AI engineers 1 / 2 · integration engineer 0.25 / 1 · domain scientist 0.25 / 0.5 · enablement lead 0.5 / 0.5, in all 2.75 / 5.25 FTE or about 18 / 63 person-weeks including prep and advisory: planning estimates for one team of 20–40. (chat, 2026-10-20)
- **What the program measures (page 1, 2026-10-20):** one outcome per bucket, the same teams before vs after, counted from records the team already keeps. 1 Know the literature: rework forced by a paper, patent or rule found after a gate (gate records) · 2 Learn from our results: experiments a campaign needs to hit its spec (ELN, LIMS) · 3 Run and capture: runs redone for missing or bad data (LIMS) · 4 Model before building: syntheses or prototypes per design that passes (ELN, PLM) · 5 Keep the paper trail: regulator questions per filing, failures traced to an unchecked change, days from lab result to invention disclosure (regulatory log, QMS, Anaqua) · 6 Share across the team: weeks until a new scientist runs an experiment alone (HR start date, ELN). (chat, 2026-10-20)
- **Partners:** only platforms big enough to bring leads: **Microsoft Discovery** (established partnership and a funded enterprise sales machine; Inna: the most relevant, partner-team requests already coming), **Claude Science**, **OpenAI's science workbench**, **NVIDIA** (AI-Q for research, open-source drug-discovery models). That is three closed-source and one open-source option. AWS and Google are still to research. The program is positioned as tied to no single partner, because Jumpstart demand came from clients' own stacks. **NobleAI is the competitor**: domain knowledge but weak, vibe-coded software and an aggressive forward-deployed-engineering model that leaves partners only cloud and program management. [sync](calls/jumpstart-science/2026-10-19_201609_default_pasted-science-jumpstart-sync.md) The program runs on the client's own AI stack (Claude, ChatGPT or Microsoft Discovery), and the kit ships for each (Alex). (chat, 2026-10-20)
- **Commercial stance:** always sell transformation, offer cheaper packages; NobleAI's offer, a 5-month engagement (target set in month 1, something working by month 5), is the starting model. Alex's Payworks lesson behind it: client success came only with transformation, which SoftServe didn't deliver there. [sync](calls/jumpstart-science/2026-10-19_201609_default_pasted-science-jumpstart-sync.md) Since 2026-10-20 that means one program in two modes (Light = training, Full = transformation) with industry kits, not separate editions; discovery is sold inside the contract, and Full commits to shared targets per pick without tying fees to them (Alex). A price per mode is still open. (chat, 2026-10-20)
- **Positioning evidence:** under Alex's inclusion rule (keep only use cases plain ChatGPT or Claude, even with Projects, can't do, or does poorly or slowly), the vendor science workbenches cover one scientist's literature work, molecular modeling and analysis. None runs a literature and patent watch, picks next experiments from run history, drives lab automation, keeps PLM/ALM records, assembles regulatory dossiers or captures team routines, and only Microsoft Discovery gives a team a shared knowledge base. That uncovered ground is the track's positioning. (chat, 2026-10-19) → [exec summary](../../../outputs/jumpstart-science/jumpstart-science-exec-summary.md)
