- **What the program measures (revised 2026-10-23 on Arek's input):** days saved can't be captured inside the program for most buckets: a result-to-decision cycle, a sign-off chain or a new hire's onboarding outlasts six or even eleven weeks, and only "finished run to checked result" turns over fast enough, in Extended on live systems. So, per bucket, **in the program a tracer task**, timed at a week-1 baseline and retested in the final week, with a quality check on its output: 1 answer recurring literature questions · citations that check out; 2 propose a next run from a past result · the analysis reproduces on rerun; 3 file and check one day of runs · runs filed complete first time; 4 re-run a past modeling request · matches the expert's own run; 5 draft a disclosure or regulator section · accepted without major edits; 6 find known past results and decisions · share found, each with its source. **After the program, days saved** (page 1, the ROI metric), read from the team's records 3 and 6 months on: 1 days to complete a literature or patent review (project tracker) · 2 days from a result to the decision on what to run next (ELN) · 3 days from a finished run to a checked result (LIMS) · 4 days from a modeling request to its result (request log, ELN) · 5 days to draft and sign off a disclosure, change assessment or regulator-file section (Anaqua, PLM, Veeva Vault) · 6 weeks until a new scientist runs an experiment alone (HR start date, ELN). **Adoption** is one team measure, weekly users of the agents, no longer one per bucket. All of these are examples: the client agrees the specific tracer tasks and metrics at the presales workshop (Alex).
