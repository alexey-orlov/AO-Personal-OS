- **2026-09-24 — How Oracle Marketplace actually works, from a partner who has sold through it for ~2 years** ([call note](calls/oracle/2026-09-24_182401_default_20260924173131F5EF99C7.md)). Alex asked Tony (TPX Impact, an Oracle-centric UK partner), with AJ present and Federico (Oracle EMEA Marketplace) joining late, how to list SoftServe's two NVIDIA-based apps and, later, non-NVIDIA packages such as AIDP. **Tony's verdict: Marketplace is a way to transact, not a way to be found.** Every paid TPX listing is **private**, because each customer gets its own deal, SOW, terms and rates. Deals come in through Oracle sellers and are then run through Marketplace to **skip long procurement** (a bank can take 6 months) and to let the customer **spend expiring Oracle cloud credits**. A public listing only makes sense for a generic, self-installable app at uniform rates. Whether a public listing works as a lead magnet (the AppExchange pattern) is untested. **Three ways to charge (as AJ laid them out):** (1) **private offer with a SOW**: paid upfront, minus a ~3% Oracle fee, no dependence on credits, best for multi-year or complex deals; (2) **ISV/product listing** with the margin on the product; (3) **VM image billed hourly** while it runs in the customer tenancy, IP + services managed-service style, paid monthly in arrears. TPX uses only (3), e.g. $125/h to burn year-end credits and a generic public POC listing at $35/h. **Constraints:** customers can usually spend only **25% of credits** on Marketplace, with exceptions through Oracle's Deal Approval System (DAS); payouts need a **US entity with a US bank account**, in USD (SoftServe has one); the publisher tenancy needs an **Ashburn region** (SoftServe is setting it up); **OCI only**: Tony had a listing rejected for mentioning Azure, so listing copy cannot name other clouds; **AIDP is an Oracle product, not an ISV listing**, so services around it go by private offer. Packaging can be Terraform, a VM image or a container. The documentation says publishing needs the GPUs provisioned in SoftServe's own tenancy, but Tony has never seen that. AJ's advice is to package pilots against **on-demand GPU capacity** and switch to a dedicated cluster for production, probably just an endpoint change (to be confirmed with Pankaj Sharma / Sanjay). **Oracle's Marketplace team gives technical/back-end help only, no GTM support.** It is small, based on the US West Coast and slow to answer.
