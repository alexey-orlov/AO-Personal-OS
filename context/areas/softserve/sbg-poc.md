## Hard problems (the technical scope drivers, in order of difficulty)

1. **Package definitions are unstable** — same name, different scope across contracts; piling ≠ piling; SBG-provided items invisible in sub BOQs; no identifiers. Name-based matching across ~34 packages, ~150 subcontracts and thousands of BOQ lines with SME adjudication is the largest single effort line. *The SOW's answer is §5.3: a unified package identifier becomes SBG's prerequisite, with quality explicitly tied to outcome.*
2. **Cost↔schedule join** — cost is by trade/package, schedule by facility/zone/building/phase. Whether P6 activities carry a package/subcontractor code was never confirmed; the SOW now converts this from a risk into a prerequisite. Still request the **combined zoning+building layout** by name.
3. **Bounded contract parsing is in** — package definition isn't knowable without BOQ + responsibility matrix; documents are scanned PDFs with handwritten amendments. Scope: the selected packages only.
4. **Root cause of variance is recorded nowhere** — decisions between award and final account are invisible. Findings are **inferred candidate drivers** with evidence and confidence, never assertions (R7 guards the misreading).
5. **Provisional sums distort everything naive** — 40–60% accuracy by design; ~SAR 1.27bn of SEVEN/Town's 5.97bn (~21%). **Still not designed into the SOW.**
6. **Reporting formats differ** — now framed as variation *within* the anchor project (R12) rather than between two projects.
