## Summary
Two founders describe how their company moved from running 20+ specialized AI agents to consolidating into a smaller set of deeper, cross-functional agents and what that means in practice. Central argument: when agents are given broad data access and are managed well (including agents managing other agents), they unlock end-to-end automation and much higher productivity, but that forces new vendor, build-vs-buy, and operational trade-offs.
