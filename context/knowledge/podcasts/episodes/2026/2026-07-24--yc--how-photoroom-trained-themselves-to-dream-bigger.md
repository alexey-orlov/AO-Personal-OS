## Summary
Founders of PhotoRoom describe how YC, deliberate practices, and team choices helped them expand what they believed was possible — from modest goals to multi‑billion visions. The episode explains cultural barriers (especially in Europe), concrete hiring and product tactics they used to scale, and simple mental exercises (e.g., add a zero) that force different planning and outcomes.
