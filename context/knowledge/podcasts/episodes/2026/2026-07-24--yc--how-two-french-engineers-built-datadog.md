## Summary
Datadog co-founder Olivier Pomel recounts how he and his co-founder turned a DevSecOps collaboration idea into a cloud-first monitoring platform that rode the explosive growth of cloud infrastructure. He explains early product positioning, fundraising friction, and the leadership practices that scaled the company (hiring/firing speed, hands-on CEO involvement). He closes with how AI is forcing rapid product and org pivots — automating much of engineering work and requiring faster, experimental bets.
