## Summary
The OpenCode CEO explains how a deliberately open, multi-model coding agent product achieved rapid global growth, why vendor blocking and cheap open-source models accelerated adoption, and how their product and unit economics work. The throughline is that a neutral, model-agnostic platform can both scale fast (consumers to enterprises) and act as a marketplace that improves choice, competition, and margins.
