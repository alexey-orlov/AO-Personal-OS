## Summary
The talk explains what gives startups long-term durability in an era when software and AI make implementation easy. Central arguments: pick problems with a genuinely hard core (technical, regulatory, or go-to-market), launch early to learn from real users, and treat AI as an amplifier while preserving human roles that can't be automated. Practical YC-backed examples and experiments illustrate when to raise capital, how to pick co-founders, and what AI can and cannot replace.
