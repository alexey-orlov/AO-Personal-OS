## Summary
This interview with Alex (founder of Ami/Amilabs) argues that the next wave of useful AI will come from 'world models' trained on raw sensory data (video, audio, robotics) rather than text-based LLMs. Alex explains why world models matter for real-world common sense and robotics, why building them requires unprecedented compute/data/talent (which drove a roughly €1bn seed), and how founders should pick narrow, tractable initial problems while keeping an audacious long-term vision.
