## Summary
An interview with Encord's cofounder (Eric) arguing that 'Physical AI'—AI that senses and manipulates the real world—is the next major platform shift. The conversation covers why robotics and autonomous systems amplify AI's economic opportunity, why data scale and specialized tooling (not just models) are the bottleneck, and practical founder lessons about product-market fit, hiring, and where to place teams and facilities.
