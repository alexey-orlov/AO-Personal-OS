## Summary
Jensen Huang recounts how Nvidia grew by learning fundamentals, confronting failure, and focusing on accelerating algorithmic domains rather than just building chips. He argues that systems thinking, rapid self-education, and an emphasis on controllable agentic systems (from software to physical robots) are the core mindset drivers that enabled Nvidia to lead in graphics, deep learning, and the emerging physical-AI era.
