## Summary
Jared Isaacman lays out an aggressive, phased plan for returning to the Moon (boots by 2028/29) and building a lunar base using iterative, high-cadence missions, commercial launchscale, humanoid robots, AI autonomy, and stepped nuclear propulsion. He argues NASA must focus on a few hard things, partner with industry for launch capacity, use AI to mine vast archival and new data, and pursue practical nuclear electric propulsion to enable repeatable crewed Mars missions.
