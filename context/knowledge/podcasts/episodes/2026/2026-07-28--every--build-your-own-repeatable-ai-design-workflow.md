## Summary
A short demo and walkthrough of Flora, a node-based creative canvas that helps designers turn image generation processes into repeatable, shareable techniques. The host and guest show how to extract a system prompt from a base image, adapt it to article content to produce multiple variations, and publish the workflow as an app for teammates. The central argument: codifying visual workflows reduces repetitive effort, speeds iteration, and makes high-concept creative work scalable across a team.
