## Summary
Founder Blake Scholl recounts Boom's decade-long effort to revive supersonic passenger travel and argues that faster, quieter supersonic flight is both technologically feasible and societally transformative. He explains how a small, vertically integrated team used software-like engineering, rapid physical iteration, and creative business models to cut cost, prove safety (including eliminating the sonic boom), change regulation, and attract funding. Interwoven are practical lessons for founders about mission, hiring, and how to learn hard technical domains.
