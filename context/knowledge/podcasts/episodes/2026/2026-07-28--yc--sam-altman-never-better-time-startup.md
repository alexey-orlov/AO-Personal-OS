## Summary
Sam Altman argues that right now is a uniquely fertile moment to start companies because AI is rapidly lowering costs and cycle times, letting small teams accomplish projects that previously required much larger organizations. He gives practical startup advice — embrace contrarian long-term convictions, find co‑founders and networks, and be helpful to others — while warning that AI also creates real safety and concentration risks that must be managed.
