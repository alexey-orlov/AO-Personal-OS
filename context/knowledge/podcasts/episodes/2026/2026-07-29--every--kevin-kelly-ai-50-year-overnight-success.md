## Summary
A wide-ranging conversation with Kevin Kelly about how the current AI wave is the visible culmination of decades of work — an overnight success that took fifty years. Kelly argues we still don't really understand intelligence, that large language models change creative workflows into editing/co-creation, that emotional bonding with AI will be profound, and that embodied technologies (VR/robots) will progress much more slowly because of physical and biological constraints.
