## Summary
The founder of Gamma explains how the company scaled from early prototype to massive product-led growth by prioritizing product-first distribution, creator relationships, and community before building a sales organization. The throughline is: make the product so immediately valuable it spreads, intensely support creators and power users, iterate by dogfooding, and only add lean, collaborative GTM teams once inbound and enterprise needs demand it.
