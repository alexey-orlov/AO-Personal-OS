## Summary
Founders of Lassie explain how they built AI agents that run the back-office work of small healthcare practices (especially dental offices), moving from manual service to largely autonomous software. Their throughline: embed in offices to capture context and build tools, then swap in better models so the software does labor rather than just store data. They argue this makes the addressable market far larger than traditional SaaS and outline the technical, operational, and go-to-market work required to scale across distributed, nontechnical SMBs.
