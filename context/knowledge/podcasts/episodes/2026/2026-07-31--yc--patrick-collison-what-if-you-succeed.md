## Summary
Patrick Collison discusses the interplay between human cognitive skill and AI, practical startup choices (dropping out, launch timing), and what Stripe's data reveals about entrepreneurship today. His throughline is that human judgment and institutional complexity still matter, AI expands opportunities rather than concentrates them, and founders should ask not only how to avoid failure but whether they'll want to live with success.
