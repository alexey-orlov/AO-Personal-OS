## Summary
Tom Verrilli argues that product management as a permanent specialist role is often a trade-off that reduces engineers' and designers' ability to make product decisions. Whatnot intentionally minimizes PM headcount, maps PMs to problems (not teams), and pushes senior PMs to remain hands-on; AI and modern data tooling make this shift feasible and productive.
