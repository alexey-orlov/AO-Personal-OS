## Summary
OpenAI engineer Nick Bowman demonstrates how he combines Codex, the ChatGPT app (Work/mobile), plugins, and a new voice layer to create always-on assistants that actually do work for him. The throughline: by letting models fork threads, see your screen, use browser sessions and plugins, and accept voice dictation, you can delegate scheduling, expenses, site-building, and even end-to-end video editing. These features change work from prompting single replies to orchestrating background workflows and continuous monitoring.
