## Summary
Former U.S. CTO Michael Kratsios explains the White House's strategy to accelerate scientific discovery using AI, combining policy changes, procurement, and new funding mechanisms. The central argument is that government must both get out of the way for "born free" technologies and actively rework rules, incentives, and infrastructure for "born in captivity" technologies so AI can transform labs, grants, and national missions—potentially doubling or even 10x scientific productivity. He lays out concrete tools (Genesis mission, long grants, fast reviews, prizes, autonomous labs, and exportable American AI stacks) to operationalize that vision.
