## Summary
Turner Caldwell, CEO of Mariana Minerals, argues that the modern economy and future technologies are fundamentally constrained by access to mined metals and critical minerals. Mariana's thesis is that Western capability was lost and must be rebuilt through vertically integrated, software-first mining and refining paired with autonomy to rapidly scale production and resilience. The company is deploying its Mariana OS and autonomy on a Utah copper asset (Copper One) as a fast-learning sandbox to prove the approach.
