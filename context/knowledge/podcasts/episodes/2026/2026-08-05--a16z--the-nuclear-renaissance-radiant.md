## Summary
The video profiles Radiant, a startup aiming to revive nuclear power by mass-producing small, transportable reactors designed to meet real customer needs. Its central argument is that the U.S. lost the ability to iterate on nuclear technology due to regulation, bad public messaging, and large site-built projects, and that factory-built microreactors can restore rapid innovation, energy security, and new use cases on Earth and in space. Radiant plans to prove the concept with a full-power test at Idaho National Laboratory and emphasizes lowering customer friction to make nuclear a commodity.
