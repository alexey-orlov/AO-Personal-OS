## Summary
The conversation argues that as base AI models and infrastructure mature, the next breakout consumer AI products will shift from single‑player, model‑centric experiences (ChatGPT/Character AI) to multiplayer, social products driven by product designers. Success will depend less on raw model performance and more on social mechanics—discoverability, reputation, trust, shared training data, and status incentives—that create network effects. The discussion covers early signals (custom GPTs, prompt libraries, AI friends), incumbent lock‑in risks (memory, habits), and investing instincts for founders who can build social, networked experiences.
