## Summary
Garry Tan argues that the next phase of AI will arrive not as a single godlike event but as diffuse, personal agents that run on your context and compound over time. He advances a practical and political case: build an owned personal AGI—your library, skill files, and harness—so your cognitive work is an asset you control rather than a rented product. He gives concrete how-to steps, product receipts from YC startups and his GBrain system, and a warning that ownership of skill files determines whether your judgment compounds for you or is extracted from you.
