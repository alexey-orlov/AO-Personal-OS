## Summary
Adam Ward explains how to hire for extreme "talent density" by abandoning mass funnels and treating each hire like a targeted executive search. He lays out a repeatable process — precise scoping, market-mapping the top candidates, relentless personalized outreach, high-signal work samples/on-sites, and continuous closing — and shows why those practices scale to build better teams over time. The throughline: quality-first, bespoke recruiting beats volume-driven funnels if talent is your company's top priority.
