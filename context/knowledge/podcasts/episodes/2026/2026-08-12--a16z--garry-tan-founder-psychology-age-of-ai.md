## Summary
Garry Tan (Y Combinator) explains how founder psychology — earnestness, courage, and choosing the right people — matters more than chasing trends, and how agentic AI is reshaping company design. He argues founders should build from unique domain knowledge, use agents to turn processes into reusable "skill files", and that societal adoption of AI will be slower than apocalyptic narratives suggest.
