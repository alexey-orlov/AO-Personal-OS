## Summary
The conversation lays out a 2x2 framework for go-to-market strategy: lighthouse versus land-grab, driven by buyer exposure and whether proof travels in the market. Guests use concrete examples (Samsara, STO, Harvey, Further AI) to show when to pursue marquee-client, proof-driven adoption versus volume-driven, math-first sales, and they give practical rules for trials, hiring and sequencing. The throughline: pick the approach that matches buyer risk and budget, then execute quickly with disciplined proofs and the right sales roles.
