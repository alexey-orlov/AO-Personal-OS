## Summary
Susan Kare recounts designing the original Macintosh icons, system fonts, and UI graphics, explaining how severe technical limits shaped clear, timeless visual metaphors. She traces specific choices—Chicago font, the Happy Mac, the command-key symbol—shows how iteration and constraint led to usable designs, and shares lessons from later commercial projects and design mentors.
