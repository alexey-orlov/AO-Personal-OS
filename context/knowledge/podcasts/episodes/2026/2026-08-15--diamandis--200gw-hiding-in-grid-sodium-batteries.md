## Summary
Ramez Naam argues that the real constraint on AI scale-up is electrical infrastructure — primarily the distribution grid and permitting — not generation. He surveys practical near-term fixes (interruptible loads, behind‑the‑meter generation, batteries) and longer‑term options (solar at scale, SMRs, fusion, and exotic siting like space or ocean datacenters). The throughline: generation is abundant or getting cheaper, but transmission, connection lead times, and regulatory incentives determine whether installed compute can actually be powered.
