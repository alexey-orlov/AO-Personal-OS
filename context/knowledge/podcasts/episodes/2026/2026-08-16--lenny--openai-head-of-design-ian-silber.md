## Summary
Ian Silber (Head of Design, OpenAI) explains why AI is simultaneously destabilizing and empowering designers: many feel anxious because expectations and workflows are shifting, but the tools also give designers unprecedented ability to prototype, iterate, and shape product experience. The throughline is that designers who embrace AI, prototype fast, and think in systems will gain outsized influence while humans remain essential for deep user understanding and novel interactions.
