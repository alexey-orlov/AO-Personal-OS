## Summary
Founder Yana explains how she built an AI-native fashion brand by using large-language and image models at every step: ideation, design, visualization, prototyping, vendor sourcing, and the website/operations. Her central argument is that AI can act as a technical co‑founder and production assistant, lowering the cost and friction of executing unusual designs while shifting remaining hard problems to pattern accuracy and fine-grained image realism.
