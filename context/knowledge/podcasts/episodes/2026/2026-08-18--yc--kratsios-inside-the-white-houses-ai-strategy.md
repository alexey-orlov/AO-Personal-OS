## Summary
Former OSTP Director Michael Kratsios explains how the White House approaches AI policy: favoring open-source ecosystems, avoiding rigid regulatory red lines, and coordinating across many agencies. He argues the government's proper role is to shape the arena (funding, infrastructure, standards) rather than dictate scientific discovery, and highlights concrete priorities like enabling startups, preventing patchwork state rules, and preparing science for AI-driven automation.
