## Summary
Jen Abel walks through a detailed, tactical enterprise-sales playbook — far fuller than the usual five-stage CRM view — showing the micro-steps that consistently win $100K+ deals. Her throughline: start top (exec or their N−1), extract an information edge on the first call, co-design demos and pilots with an internal champion, and tightly manage papering and procurement to close.
