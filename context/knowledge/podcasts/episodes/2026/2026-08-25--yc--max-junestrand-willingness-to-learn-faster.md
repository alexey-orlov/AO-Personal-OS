## Summary
Max Junestrand tells the origin story and scaling playbook of Legora, a legal-AI startup that went from a small Swedish team to explosive ARR growth by learning the market, prioritizing product delivery over model ownership, and building a hard-charging culture. The throughline: domain knowledge can be acquired quickly if you obsess over customers, pick the right people, and apply disciplined product and sales decisions.
