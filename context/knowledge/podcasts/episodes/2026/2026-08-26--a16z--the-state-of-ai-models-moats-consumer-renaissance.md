## Summary
The talk surveys where AI is going next: market structure, how models translate into products, and the sudden consumer opportunity. The central argument is that compute supply constraints and domain-specialized models create durable opportunities at the application layer, while consumer-facing personal agents and lower-cost open-weight models are catalyzing a renaissance in paid consumer AI products.
