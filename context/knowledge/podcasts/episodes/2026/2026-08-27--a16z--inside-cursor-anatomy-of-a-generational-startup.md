## Summary
Investors and operators dissect Cursor's product, go-to-market, hiring, and M&A choices that turned it from an IDE experiment into a dominant developer platform. The throughline: disciplined, product-first decisions (even when contrarian) combined with ruthless hiring, targeted acquisitions, and a willingness to evolve the product stack carried Cursor past much larger incumbents. The episode traces concrete inflection points—when they resisted plugins, delayed enterprise sales, built a top-tier AE engine, and used acquisitions to import founders and talent.
