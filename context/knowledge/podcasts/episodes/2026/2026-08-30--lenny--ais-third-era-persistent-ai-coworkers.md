## Summary
Tara Seshan explains how AI is entering a third era where persistent agents become co‑workers, shifting knowledge work from doing to directing. She argues product teams must move faster, build for rapidly improving models, and redesign interfaces so humans can verify process and reasoning — not just check outputs.
