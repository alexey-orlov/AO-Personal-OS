## Summary
The conversation argues that AI demand is already outpacing available compute capacity and that the next few years will be defined more by chronic under‑supply than by overbuild. That shortage reshapes business models: labs can choose to monetize inference or reallocate capacity to training (which changes reported revenue), financing structures and Nvidia's ecosystem will determine who scales, and alternatives like open source and orbital compute will influence access and inequality.
