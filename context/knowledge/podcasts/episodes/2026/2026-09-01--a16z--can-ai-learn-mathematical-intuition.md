## Summary
An interview with Daniel about what current large language models can and cannot do in mathematical research. The central argument is that LLMs are already strong at applying known techniques, grinding computations, and producing human-like chains of thought, but they struggle with fuzzy theory-building, developing deep intuition, and the high-level conceptual analogies that often drive new mathematics. The guest expects capabilities to improve with better training/harnesses and curricula, but warns the community must adapt incentives to preserve human learning and avoid low‑quality, automated 'slot‑machine' research.
