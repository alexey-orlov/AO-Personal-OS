## Summary
Moderna (with Merck) announced a positive phase‑3 result for an individualized mRNA cancer vaccine given with pembrolizumab (Keytruda) in melanoma, marking the first time a cancer vaccine beat Keytruda alone in this setting. The conversation explains why mRNA plus deep personalization worked where previous cancer‑vaccine efforts failed, how Moderna has industrialized manufacture for one‑patient‑at‑a‑time production, and the company's regulatory and clinical plans to expand into other cancers and disease areas.
