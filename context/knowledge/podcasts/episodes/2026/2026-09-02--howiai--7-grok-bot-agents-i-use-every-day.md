## Summary
A product tour and practical playbook for using Grockbot (an agent platform) through seven real agents the creator runs daily. The central argument: Grockbot's simple UI, multi-account connectors, and per-bot VM+routine model make it especially effective for automating recurring, cross-tool workflows at work and at home. It can replace many repetitive human tasks today, though it lacks native multiplayer/group-chat agent features. The video demonstrates specific, repeatable agent designs for chief-of-staff, family coordination, engineering ops, helpdesk, shopping, and finance automation.
