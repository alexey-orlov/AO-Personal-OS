## Summary
Two veterans of payments and consumer finance trace how structural limits in payments infrastructure and shifts in merchant economics enabled buy‑now‑pay‑later (BNPL) to scale, and argue that AI agents — not purely new shopping bots — are poised to upend the credit‑card user interface. They use Affirm's origin story and merchant case studies (beauty sites, mattresses) to show why underwriting, merchant willingness to pay MDRs, and long customer lifecycles matter more than gimmicks. Finally they forecast agents changing payments UX (and settling some longstanding frictions) while being cautious about fully agentic shopping.
