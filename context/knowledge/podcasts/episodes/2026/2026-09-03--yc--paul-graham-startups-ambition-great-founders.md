## Summary
Paul Graham explains what makes founders succeed: an often-inborn ambition, a relentless habit of shipping, and being 'formidable' — people who get what they want. He also discusses how AI surprised expectations by appearing human-like before being perfected, and how these changes affect startup economics and tactics.
