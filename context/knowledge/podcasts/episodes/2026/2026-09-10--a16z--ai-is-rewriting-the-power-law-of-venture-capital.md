## Summary
The conversation argues that AI is magnifying the venture-capital power law by making capital and compute directly compounding competitive advantages, expanding total addressable markets far beyond historical SaaS estimates, and concentrating returns in a tiny set of firms that capture category winners. As a result LP/GP incentives, fund sizing, early-stage access, and portfolio construction all need to be rethought — and supply-side bottlenecks (energy, data centers, chips) are now a critical constraint and investment opportunity.
