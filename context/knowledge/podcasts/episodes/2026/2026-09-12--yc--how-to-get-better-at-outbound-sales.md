## Summary
A practical guide for founders on how to run effective outbound sales: who to target, how to write messages that get replies, and how to iterate using measurable feedback. The central argument is that success comes from precise targeting (right person and right company), concise, credibility-focused messaging, disciplined follow-up, and using reply data and customer language to improve. Doing the founder-led outreach consistently pays off because founders learn signals no outside salesperson can.
