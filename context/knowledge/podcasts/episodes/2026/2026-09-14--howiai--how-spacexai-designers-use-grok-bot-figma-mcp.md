## Summary
Designers from SpaceX AI demonstrate how Grokbot and a Figma MCP let them build end-to-end pipelines, prototypes, and production assets without deep engineering work. The throughline is that low-friction AI agents compress discovery-to-delivery: a designer can describe an idea, have the bot wire up APIs, generate assets, and publish or iterate quickly. That lowers the cost of experimentation, democratizes specialist creative skills, and frees designers to work at a higher strategic level.
