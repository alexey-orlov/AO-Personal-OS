## Summary
Martine Rothblatt explains her parallel moonshots: manufacturing unlimited human organs, rejuvenating the thymus to extend healthy lifespan, and preparing legal/ethical frameworks for conscious digital minds. She argues these are practical, near-term engineering problems being pursued with multiple independent approaches, several already in clinical testing, and that AI-driven digital personhood and large-scale organ supply will arrive in the 2030s. The throughline is a pragmatic "multiple shots on goal" strategy — pursue several scientifically plausible routes, scale what works, and prioritize interventions that can affect billions of people.
