## Summary
The host walks through Muse, Meta's consumer-facing personal agent, arguing that its UX and product primitives make it the most approachable personal agent yet. The central claim is that Muse prioritizes trust, delightful design, and consumer mental models (apps, goals, library) over raw technical showmanship, which makes it more useful for everyday personal tasks like family agendas, goals, and local errands.
