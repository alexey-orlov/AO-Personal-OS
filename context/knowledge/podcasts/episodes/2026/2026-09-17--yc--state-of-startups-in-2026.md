## Summary
A group of YC partners review how AI and agentic tooling have reshaped startup economics in 2026, driving a revival of hardware ('atoms') companies, changing how SaaS is built and sold, and creating new high-revenue data businesses. Their throughline: powerful models reduce software engineering bottlenecks, enable faster scientific progress, and make full end‑to‑end, agent-driven products and physical infrastructure startups far more viable than a few years ago.
