## Summary
The hosts explain how they re-architected their revenue stack around purpose-built agents (a central 10K agent plus headless Salesforce) to automate inbound, renewals, and parts of outbound motion. Their throughline: combine first-party data, heat-maps, enrichment, and automated personalized content (decks/emails/bookers) so agents can handle scale and hand off high-context opportunities to humans. The result: rapid, measurable lift in sponsorship revenue and renewals and a repeatable playbook for other B2B sellers.
