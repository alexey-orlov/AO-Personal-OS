## Summary
Peter Sellis (former product leader at Snap and Discord) gives blunt, tactical product and organization advice: hire and structure teams to minimize costly collaboration, raise the bar for who counts as a PM, and focus growth efforts on improving core daily usage. He uses examples from Snap (camera-first, teen-heavy user base) and Discord (gaming/voice focus) to show how product shape determines monetization and where to prioritize engineering and product effort.
