## Summary
Dan Shipper argues product teams must add small, dedicated research 'labs' to survive rapid AI-driven change. Labs explore many parallel experiments (most discarded) while the core product team focuses on scaling proven ideas; a clear pipeline and decision criteria move winners from lab to product. He gives concrete structure, team roles, signals (internal usage, 10x improvement, cost-to-scale) and real examples from Every and the wider AI industry.
