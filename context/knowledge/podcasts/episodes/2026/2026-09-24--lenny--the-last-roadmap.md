## Summary
Claire Vo argues that traditional feature-by-date product roadmaps are becoming dangerous because execution has become cheap while conviction about what matters has not. With abundant engineering and AI agents, teams can build anything, but that amplifies three failure modes; instead leaders should prioritize testing strong convictions, run bold experiments, and map ambition rather than features. The talk gives concrete examples from her work and prescribes a shift from output schedules to proof-driven investments and clear customer promises.
