## Summary
Marty Cagan walks through ten major regrets since publishing Inspired, arguing that product practice has under-emphasized several load-bearing issues: commercial and organizational feasibility, the balance between problem discovery and solution discovery, the corrosive effects of overconfidence embodied in roadmaps/PRDs, the need for stronger product leadership, and the imperative to think (not just follow processes). He concludes that AI increases both the urgency and the opportunity for discovery-driven product work — especially by enabling a focus on outcomes and a "build to learn" posture.
