## Summary
Katie Dill argues that generative AI makes building faster but also risks producing generic, context-less products unless teams are deliberate. She gives four practical prescriptions: hold a clear point of view, encode standards into production systems, preserve editorial quality after rapid building, and use AI to raise creative ambition. Concrete examples from Stripe (a CLI tied to their design system, a magazine cover workflow, a 56-attempt animation) show how to make AI outputs coherent and soulful rather than 'zombie' interfaces.
