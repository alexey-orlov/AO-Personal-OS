## Summary
A panel dissects the current AI safety debate, arguing that the dominant ''slow the pace'' narrative is both misleading and politically dangerous. They shift attention to concrete security engineering problems (agent swarms, API/auth tracking, internal attack surfaces) and to the idea that the most important product and software innovation will happen outside the big model labs at the application and tooling layer.
