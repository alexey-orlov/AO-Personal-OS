## Summary
This is an interview with Molly Graham re-evaluating her long‑running "give away the LEGO" career advice in the age of AI. Graham argues the core lesson — embrace change and prioritize learning over clinging to roles — still holds, but AI complicates the tradeoffs because delegating to models doesn't remove oversight and can magnify grief, loneliness, and burnout. She urges leaders to preserve human judgment, set clear accountability for AI outputs, and treat this transition as a human problem as much as a technical one.
