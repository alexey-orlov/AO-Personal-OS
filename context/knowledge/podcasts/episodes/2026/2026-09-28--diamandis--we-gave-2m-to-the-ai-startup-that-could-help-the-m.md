## Summary
The video covers the Gemini X Prize event where judges (including Palmer Luckey, Cathie Wood, Logan Kilpatrick, and Marc Pincus) awarded $2M in non-dilutive capital to early AI startups that aim to help large numbers of people. The discussion frames a new 'social contract' for graduates—build mission-driven startups using AI—while judges debate where AI will have the biggest impact (healthcare, data infrastructure, social interaction). The program's point was both practical (show teams can build revenue-generating companies in 90 days) and demonstrative (inspire others to found AI-first companies).
