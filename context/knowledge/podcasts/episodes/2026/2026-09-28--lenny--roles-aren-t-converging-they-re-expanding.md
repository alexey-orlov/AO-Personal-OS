## Summary
Tamar Yehoshua argues that product roles are not disappearing under AI but expanding: people are becoming more 'builders' who mix design, product and engineering tasks aided by AI and organizational context. She shows how Atlassian teams changed how they work—using agents, prototyping pipelines, and contribution models—to accelerate delivery while keeping enterprise safety and scale. The throughline: PMs still own product-market fit, but must adopt new hands-on skills and orchestration practices to speed outcomes.
