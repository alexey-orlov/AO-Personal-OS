## Summary
Elena Verna explains why the next career archetype is the high-impact individual contributor (HI-IC): a single person who can identify a business problem, execute across functions, publish results, and iterate. She argues AI and cheaper building reduce the cost of execution relative to coordination, so companies must reorganize (information access, decision authority, compensation) to capture this change. Elena uses her move from growth leader to IC at Lovable as a worked example of the benefits and necessary organizational changes.
