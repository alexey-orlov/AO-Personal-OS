## Summary
Robby Stein explains the modern craft of product management: the unique value of a PM is making great judgments and decisions, built on deep user understanding, ruthless root-cause diagnosis, and obsessive polish. He shows how those skills produced wins at Instagram (Stories, Reels) and are now amplified by AI to scale user research, QA, and delightful experience details in Google Search.
