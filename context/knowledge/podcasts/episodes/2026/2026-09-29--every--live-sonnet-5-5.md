## Summary
The hosts test Anthropic's Sonnet 5.5 and argue it is a surprising step up from previous Sonnet models — especially on low and medium effort settings — making it a practical, faster, and cheaper alternative to Opus for iterative design and engineering workflows. Their throughline: Sonnet 5.5 democratizes day-to-day creative and tooling work by enabling many more quick iterations, while Opus remains the better choice for high-effort, tool-heavy, or one-shot feature development.
