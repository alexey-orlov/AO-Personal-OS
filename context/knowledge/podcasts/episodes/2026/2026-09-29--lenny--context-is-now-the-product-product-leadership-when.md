## Summary
Karri Saarinen argues that as AI automates more software execution, the enduring product becomes the context and judgment that guide decisions rather than raw code output. Teams must preserve and surface customer signals, train collective taste, and use AI to aggregate learning — then reinvest saved execution time into customer contact, quality, and shared judgment rituals.
