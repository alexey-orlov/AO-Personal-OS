## Summary
A panel of Anthropic product leaders argue that large models like Claude are powerful aids but cannot yet replace product managers. The discussion traces how the PM role is evolving — more emphasis on coordination, rapid experimentation, infrastructure, and judgement — because models change every few months and products must integrate human and agent users. They give concrete examples of where AI helps and where human-led decisions, accountability, and product primitives remain necessary.
