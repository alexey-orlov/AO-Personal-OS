## Summary
The conversation argues that AI is driving a broad, economy‑wide investment boom: hyperscalers are racing to build compute, which is cascading demand into chips, power, construction and skilled labor, and creating an industrial-style capex cycle. At the same time, falling inference costs, agentic workflows, and rapid private‑market growth are unlocking new application and consumer opportunities even though enterprise adoption and measurable impact remain early.
