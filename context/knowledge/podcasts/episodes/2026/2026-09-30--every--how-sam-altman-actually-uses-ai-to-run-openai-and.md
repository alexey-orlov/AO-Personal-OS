## Summary
This is an interview with Sam Altman at OpenAI's DevDay about how he personally uses AI and how OpenAI is thinking about platform strategy, developers, and product trade-offs. Altman argues that personal agents, live AI-collaborative documents, and making high-performance models both faster and cheaper will enable a renaissance of creators and startups rather than centralize power. The conversation mixes concrete product examples (Dots, live dashboards, Decisions API / UltraFast) with a broader thesis that AI will expand human creativity and entrepreneurship.
