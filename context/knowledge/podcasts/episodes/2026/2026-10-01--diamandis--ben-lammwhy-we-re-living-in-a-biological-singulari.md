## Summary
Ben Lamm (founder of Colossal) explains how advances in synthetic biology, genome engineering, DNA synthesis and AI are pushing us toward a near-term "biological singularity" where designing and growing new organisms becomes routine. He argues Colossal's de‑extinction work (mammoths, thylacines, etc.) is both a pathway to technological capability (sequencing, editing, gestation outside the womb) and a conservation strategy that must be paired with ethical, indigenous and ecological governance.
