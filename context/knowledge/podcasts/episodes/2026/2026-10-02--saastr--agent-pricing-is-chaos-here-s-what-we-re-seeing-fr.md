## Summary
Hosts analyze data from 22,000 paid attendees of Saster AI events and explain how buyer-side dynamics are reshaping agent usage, pricing, and vendor selection. Key findings: explosive growth in AI-specific roles, very high buyer turnover, vendor attempts to charge agents for API/access, and fast-moving consumer AI tools (like Muse) already displacing pricier B2B workflows. The throughline: vendors' pricing decisions around agent access will determine which platforms survive, because buyers and agents can and will work around punitive fees.
