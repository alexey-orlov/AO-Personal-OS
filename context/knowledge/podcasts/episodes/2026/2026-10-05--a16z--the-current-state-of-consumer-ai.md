## Summary
A16z hosts a deep update on consumer-facing AI products, arguing the biggest current shift is toward personal AI agents that act on users' behalf rather than merely answer questions. The guests review adoption and spending data, ad monetization, and why long-term value will accrue to products that wrap models into rich software and contextual data rather than models alone.
