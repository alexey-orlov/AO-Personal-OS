## The throughline
At the frontier, three layers move at once. (1) Algorithmic gains — speculative decoding (SSD: parallelizes draft and verify, ~80–90% hit rates, hundreds of tokens/sec for large Llama models), diffusion MPC (multi-step action proposals with fewer compounding errors, simple sampling-based planners outperform many prior methods), latent world-model regularizers (Sigg: ~15M-parameter models running ~50× faster than competitors, with measurable out-of-distribution uncertainty signals), ensembling + distillation (5× effective data efficiency at 200M pretraining tokens; 3.7× with a practical 5-member 1B-parameter ensemble) — produce capability per dollar that bigger models alone can't. The benchmark crown flips monthly (Opus 4.8 leads AI index by ~1.2 points over GPT-5.5, SWEBench Pro 69.2 vs 58.6). LLMs are now solving research-level math: a recent Erdős conjecture disproof via novel algebraic-geometry techniques surprised leading mathematicians including Timothy Gowers. Biology follows the same scaling law: ESM Cambrian trained on 2.8B metagenomic sequences approaches specialized MSA methods for antibody design (~50 vs AlphaFold3's ~47), unlocking emergent structure representations previously blocked by data shortage. (2) Cognition is getting drastically cheaper (token prices ~$0.50/M — down ~75% since late 2024 — yet usage has risen to ~25T tokens/month; Jevons paradox on steroids), but the real cycle constraint is supply: compute, memory, data centers, and power bind into 2028–2029. Record single-data-center compute has doubled roughly every seven months since Colossus One; global AI compute capacity expands ~3.3× per year; the named bottleneck has shifted from chips to electrical infrastructure (2.5–3 year transformer lead times). SpaceX IPOs at $2.89T pricing a triple-exponential thesis (launch monopoly + Starlink + orbital compute) and signed an $11B/year Google GPU deal — turning itself into a hyperscaler. Evans's mobile-data analogy: today's capacity crunch and surprise bills look exactly like the early mobile-data era — capex will surge into hundreds of billions to low trillions before telco-style low margins normalize the model layer. Wind and solar exceeded natural gas globally (22% vs 20%, April 2026) as the slow underwriting of that compute demand. (3) The labs are turning into capital-and-distribution superstructures: ChatGPT crossed 1B MAU faster than any app ever (62% YoY growth; Claude at ~56M but +640% YoY), Anthropic filed at trillion-dollar implied caps, the OpenAI Foundation now controls ~$130–260B that could fund UBI/UBC experiments. AGI timelines remain contested: Demis Hassabis projected ~2029 with an "Einstein-1901 test" as the threshold; the hosts push back that some generality arguably already exists, and benchmarks are saturating. Evans's "1997 internet" frame is the connective tissue: real and transformative but jagged — humility about timelines is warranted. At the edges: Bitcoin moves toward digital-gold status (Armstrong: ~30% store-of-value vs 70% risk-asset today, shifting over time; banks publishing bullish multi-year targets); quantum computing threatens ECDSA — BIP 360 and governance questions about Satoshi-era coins remain open; Fadell predicts voice-first slabs with displays retained for glanceable surfaces; Verve-102 (single-infusion, ~62% LDL / ~88% PCSK9 reduction, 18 months durable) and NewLimit's epigenetic reprogramming ($435M, human trials next year) are the longevity-biotech frontier data points; AI is overhauling cybersecurity toward autonomous agents replacing human-in-the-loop oversight.

## Insights

### The current cycle is supply-constrained, not a classic demand-driven bubble
Rather than excess supply, the ecosystem lacks compute, memory, data center capacity, and power — the speakers expect data-center capacity shortages to persist into late 2028–2029 and believe this scarcity reduces immediate bubble risk. That constraint affects who can scale model training/inference and bolsters incumbents with access to capacity, while also making infrastructure and chip supply critical investment areas. A major algorithmic breakthrough that drastically reduces token consumption could reverse this dynamic, but absent that, limited supply shapes which firms can capitalize on AI demand.

### As token prices plunged, usage exploded — Jevons paradox on steroids
Since late 2024 token prices fell roughly 75% (from about $2 per million tokens to $0.50), yet consumption rose to ~25 trillion tokens/month, showing that cheaper cognition drives far more demand rather than savings.
