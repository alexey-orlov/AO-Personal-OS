## Top items to study this session

#### 1. 🟡 Missing articles — 4 instances

You reliably drop "a/the" before singular countable nouns and in stock phrases. It never blocks meaning but it's the most consistent non-native marker in this call.

- "**As per** description from Roberto" → **Based on the** description from Roberto
- "I got **absolutely different understanding**" → I got **a completely different understanding**
- "whatever **person uses**" → whatever **a person uses**

**Drill:** before any singular countable noun (*understanding, description, payout, person*), pause for "a / the / my". If none fits, only then say it bare.

#### 2. 🟡 Non-native verb + noun collocations — 3 instances

The verbs are close but not the ones a native speaker pairs with these nouns. Swap in the standard collocation.

- "do they **realize any struggle**" → do they **experience any pain points**
- "did I **get it correct**" → did I **get it right**
- "**absolutely different**" → **completely different**

**Drill:** memorize the fixed pairs — *experience/run into pain points*, *get it right*, *completely/totally different*. "Realize" = become aware of a fact, not "have a difficulty".

#### 3. 🟡 Indirect question — no subject-verb inversion — 1 instance

Inside a statement, a question keeps normal word order (subject before verb). You inverted it as if it were still a direct question.

- "it's not that clear **what's the missing gap**" → it's not that clear **what the gap is**

**Drill:** direct = "What is the gap?" → embedded = "…what the gap **is**." The verb moves to the end.

#### 4. 🟡 Over-long questions — the ask is buried — 2 instances

Two of your questions run 40+ words with the real question landing only at the very end ("Do you know about that?"). A tighter version helps the listener answer.

- "Question to you… do they realize any struggle around missing context or projects are isolated… Or their current status quo, they are super happy… Do you know about that?" → "Do they feel any pain today — isolated projects, missing company-wide context — or are they happy with the status quo?"

**Drill:** lead with the question, then add the options. If a listener has to wait past clause three to hear what you're asking, split it.
