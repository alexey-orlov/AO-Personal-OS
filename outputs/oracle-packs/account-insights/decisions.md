# Decisions — Account Insights pack

_One line per decision, newest last. Dates are the day the owner decided._

## The pack brief (2026-09-22)

- **Which shape the pack is** — the engagement itself moved to signal → opportunity records, so the signed statement of work is a stale document, not a competing product; and Vlad's version was to be widened, not adopted as-is. (Alex)
- **Customer name** — internal only; anonymized as "a global logistics and supply-chain operator" everywhere else. No figures cleared; the engagement has no results yet, so every metric prints "results to follow". (Alex)
- **Proof of value** — 6–8 weeks, narrower than the engagement's 12; price left open rather than invented. (Alex)
- **The pack's story** — "Account Insights", positioned on the join nobody ships: a market event → a named service line the customer actually sells, plus the effect on the other accounts in their book. Chosen over a governance-led story and over keeping Vlad's scope unchanged. (Alex)
- **The Oracle/NVIDIA baseline column** — Vlad's one-pager credited it with one capability; the research puts it at far more. Corrected quietly in the statuses, not made into the sales argument. (Alex)
- **Who buys it** — "any organization selling a catalog of services into a named book of accounts it has to stay current on". (Alex)
- **The fourth industry** — private equity stays, scoped to portfolio monitoring only; the deal-screening example is dropped because a fund screening targets has nothing to map an opportunity onto. Consequence: that industry sells at the Integration package, not the proof of value, until the mapping target is configurable. (Alex)
- **Problem and solution rewritten** — the first version named an activity ("commercial teams work out what a development means…"); the owner's test is a role and the concrete things on their desk. Now: an account manager who cannot keep up with the signals, updates and filings landing on dozens of accounts. (Alex: "kind of waterish")
- **Workflow** — six steps, not twelve; mechanics named inside a step. (Alex: "too detailed")
- **Oracle products** — the platform plus the dedicated AI cluster the engine runs on; everything else on the architecture, not in the products list. Oracle CX stays optional even though it carries the anchor story, by the owner's own precedent on the workforce pack. (Alex: "I doubt that we need that much")
