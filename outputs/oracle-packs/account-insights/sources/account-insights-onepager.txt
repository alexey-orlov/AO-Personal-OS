AI Signal-Impact Engine
- Accelerator Pack
Scope. The AI Signal-Impact Engine turns a real-world signal (a news article, filing or disclosure) into structured, machine-
readable JSON that identifies the concrete business opportunities and risks the signal creates for the client - per affected
account, not a narrative summary. For each in-scope account it reasons "so what" for that account's business, derives
candidate opportunities and flags material risks (each mapped to a specific client service line where relevant), foresees
descriptive second-order and cross-account ripples (suppliers, customers, competitors; up to two levels), scores each item by
magnitude and confidence, and cites the source evidence. The reasoning is grounded in the client's own data (CRM and
account framing, capability catalog, public filings), with a human review step. Output is one JSON per affected account that
flows into downstream sales systems; the engine informs decisions, it does not act on them. It detects both the opportunities
a signal creates and the risks it poses, so account teams can pursue upside and get ahead of downside.

Where it applies. Any business that needs to turn market and customer developments into pursuable opportunities across its
account base, quickly. Across industries, for example:

 1. Logistics & supply chain. For example:
        A signal about a shipper (plant expansion, new-market entry) turned into a concrete logistics opportunity
        (warehousing, forwarding).
        A disruption (strike, port closure, supplier fire) turned into an inbound / mitigation opportunity across affected accounts.

 2. Financial services & banking. For example:
        A signal about a counterparty or portfolio company turned into a relationship / coverage opportunity for the account team.
        Cross-holding ripples: how one issuer's event opens opportunities with related names.
 3. Industrial & manufacturing. For example:
        A signal at a supplier or OEM customer turned into supply-chain and reallocation opportunities across the account base.
        Second-order effects on production sites and trade lanes, each tied to an opportunity.
 4. Private equity funds. For example:
        A market or regulatory signal turned into thesis-relevant opportunities across portfolio companies.
        Event-driven screening of pipeline targets.

    USE-CASE BOUNDARIES
The boundary of this solution is signal-to-opportunity reasoning delivered as structured, human-reviewed JSON - one
per affected account.


   IN SCOPE

    1. Ingesting a signal plus first-party context (CRM / account framing), the service catalog and the in-scope account list
    2. Filtering for genuine signals and identifying which in-scope accounts are affected (de-duplicating one story across sources into
       one signal)
    3. Reasoning "so what" per account, deriving candidate opportunities and flagging material risks, each mapped to a concrete
       service line where relevant
    4. Foreseeing descriptive second-order / cross-account ripples (≤2 levels); an affected in-scope account gets its own JSON
    5. Scoring each opportunity (magnitude + confidence) and citing evidence; emitting one JSON per affected account




   The system produces scored, cited reasoning - both opportunities and risks - for a human to review; it informs decisions
   and downstream systems, it does not act on them.



   OUT OF SCOPE

    1. Acting on the opportunities - auto-outreach, CRM task creation or workflow automation; decisioning is a downstream step.
    2. Validating against the client's existing pipeline - checking candidates against known/existing opportunities to avoid
       suggesting deals already in flight; a post-PoC step.
    3. Monetary sizing & interactive Q&A - putting a currency value on an opportunity, or conversational follow-up over the output.
    4. Live integration & persistence - live CRM / sales-system write-back, and a persistent historical signal store + feedback loop.
    5. Deep financial / quantitative modeling - valuation, credit scoring or forecasting engines; the engine reasons over disclosures,
       it does not compute them.
    6. Native multilingual processing - single-language primary; more languages are a future extension.


The engine is a non-deterministic reasoning system, so a dedicated evaluation plan (correctness and confidence calibration) is part of
the work.
